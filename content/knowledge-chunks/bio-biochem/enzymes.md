# Enzymes — MCAT Foundations

## Kinetics
Michaelis-Menten: v = Vmax[S]/(Km + [S]). Km inversely related to affinity. Lineweaver-Burk plots help identify inhibition type.

## Inhibition
Competitive: increases apparent Km, Vmax unchanged (more substrate can overcome). Noncompetitive: lowers Vmax, Km unchanged. Uncompetitive: both Km and Vmax decrease.

## Regulation
Allosteric enzymes show sigmoid kinetics. Phosphorylation and compartmentalization are common regulatory themes.

## Traps
- Confusing competitive vs noncompetitive on graphs
- Assuming inhibitors always bind active site

## Passage Tip
Experimental passages often test whether students can predict effect on Km/Vmax from mechanism description.
