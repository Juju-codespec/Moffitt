# Circuits — MCAT Foundations

## Ohm's Law
V = IR. Power P = IV = I²R = V²/R.

## Series vs Parallel
Series: same current, resistances add. Parallel: same voltage across branches; 1/Req = Σ(1/Ri).

## Kirchhoff
Sum of currents into a junction = sum out. Loop rule: sum of voltage changes around closed loop = 0.

## Capacitors
Q = CV. In DC steady state, capacitor acts as open circuit. RC charging: exponential time constant τ = RC.

## Traps
- Treating capacitors as short circuits at steady state
- Forgetting power dissipation units

## Strategy
Redraw messy circuits. Label nodes. Identify series/parallel chunks before full analysis.
