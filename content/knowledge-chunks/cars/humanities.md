# CARS Humanities — Strategy Foundations

## Passage Approach
Spend ~3 minutes mapping structure: thesis, evidence, counterpoints, conclusion. Underline shift words (however, nevertheless).

## Question Types
Main idea: answer must be narrow enough to be supported, broad enough to capture scope. Inference: stay inside passage boundaries.

## Tone
Identify author's attitude via word choice, not your opinion. Tone answers often eliminate extremes.

## Timing
~10 minutes per passage set including questions. Flag Roman numeral and detail-heavy questions.

## Traps
- Answers that are true but not the main point
- Out-of-scope inferences
- Extreme language (always, never) unless passage supports it
